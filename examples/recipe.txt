Basic Pancakes (makes 8)

1 1/2 cups  all-purpose flour
3 1/2 tsp   baking powder
1 tbsp      sugar
1/4 tsp     salt
1 1/4 cups  milk
1           egg
3 tbsp      butter, melted

1. Whisk the flour, baking powder,
   sugar, and salt in a large bowl.
2. Add the milk, egg, and butter.
   Stir until just combined; a few
   lumps are fine.
3. Heat a lightly oiled pan over
   medium-high heat.
4. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter
   per pancake.
5. Flip when bubbles form on top
   and the edges look dry.
6. Cook until golden, about 1 min.
